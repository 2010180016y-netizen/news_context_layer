News Context Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 12, 2026. This policy explains what News Context processes, why it is processed, and how users can request help or privacy review during the current alpha.

Who operates this extension

Operator: News Context operator, an independent software project.

Public contact page: https://2010180016y-netizen.github.io/news_context_layer/public/public-contact.html

What the extension does

News Context opens a side panel beside a supported Naver News desktop article and helps users compare related coverage from other publishers.

Data we process

  • article URL or canonical URL
  • article title, publisher, author names, timestamps, section, and keywords
  • a limited article excerpt used only for comparison and related-coverage matching
  • parse confidence, freshness, and related matching signals
  • anonymous device identifiers, watchlist state, and plan state
  • optional analytics events only when the user explicitly opts in

How we use data

We use the minimum article and product state needed to show related coverage, maintain watchlists, restore limited side-panel state, and improve reliability. We do not use article data or usage data for targeted advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

Analytics

Analytics is optional and opt-in. If enabled, it is used only to improve product quality and reliability. If disabled, analytics events are not sent.

Local storage

The extension stores limited local state in the browser, such as onboarding completion, analytics preference, anonymous device identifiers, latest panel context, and checkout return state.

Data sharing

Data needed for comparison is sent to the News Context backend so related coverage can be resolved. The current alpha does not use article or usage data for advertising networks or data resale.

Data retention

Local browser state remains on the device until the user clears extension or browser storage. Operational and quality-review records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for alpha support, debugging, abuse prevention, and legal obligations. Retention and deletion review requests can be made through the public contact route below.

Deletion and privacy requests

To request deletion or privacy review, use the public contact page and include the minimum details needed to identify the request, such as the article URL, approximate time, and anonymous device identifier if available. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. If sensitive follow-up is needed, ask for a private reply path.

Public contact

The current public contact route is the News Context public contact page, which links to the project's public issue tracker for product, billing, parser-quality, and privacy inquiries.

Your choices

Users can choose whether to opt in to analytics, stop using the extension at any time, and contact the operator using the public contact route listed above.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the alpha evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this public page with an updated effective date.
